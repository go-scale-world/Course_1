# Standard Operating Playbook
## Course Operations Manual

**Course Name:** Agentic AI course on enterprsie stack
**Course Launch Date:** 15/08/26

---

## 1. Overview

This playbook defines how this course operates — how sessions are run, how materials are organised, and how assessments are handled. It is intended for instructors, TAs, and course administrators.

- Each session is **3 hours long**
- **2 sessions** are conducted per week
- All sessions are delivered **in-person**
- The course is divided into **Modules**, each covering a specific topic area

---

## 2. Course Modules

The course content is organised into 6 modules. Each module contains three folders:

**Instructor**
- `session_lecture_notes` — detailed notes to guide the instructor through each session
- `slides` — presentation slides used during delivery

**Students**
- `notes` — topic summary shared with students after each in-person session
- `lab_notes` — summary of the lab session shared with students after the lab

---

## 3. How Sessions Work

**In-Person Sessions**
- Distribute `skeleton_notes` to students at the start of the session
- Instructor runs the session using `slides` and `lecture_notes`
- Share `notes` (topic summary) with students after the session ends

**Lab Sessions**
- Practical, hands-on application of module concepts
- Share `lab_notes` with students after the lab concludes

---

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Instructor** : (Dev Agnihotri) — delivers sessions, manages lecture notes and slides
- **Student** — attends sessions, fills in skeleton notes during session, reviews summary notes after.
- Other roles are to be determined and allocated
