# Capstone Projects

## Imad — Parle-G Biscuit Supply Chain Management

Parle-G biscuits are widely consumed, so managing their distribution involves many steps.

Imad will build an **Agentic AI-based supply chain management system** for Parle-G biscuit distribution. The system will help manage the distribution process and generate reports with useful insights and recommendations.

## Akmal — Leather Supply Chain Management

The leather industry often depends on manual processes, which can take a lot of time and may lead to errors.

Akmal will build a **supply chain management system for a leather company**. The system will cover the process from receiving raw skin, processing it into leather, manufacturing, and finally supplying the finished leather to clients.

## Lavi — Persona-Based News Platform

News platforms are starting to create separate experiences for younger audiences, such as inext from Jagran brand. A personalized news experience can help users see news that is more relevant to them.

Lavi will build a **persona-based news filtering and presentation system**, that can act as a feature on a website. It will understand a user's interests or persona and filter and present news based on what is more relevant to that user.

## Suhani — FICCI Funding Recommendation System

FICCI supports businesses and organisations through different programs and initiatives, including helping connect eligible organisations with funding opportunities. Reviewing applications and deciding which organisations are a good fit can take time and may involve manual assessment.

Suhani will build an **AI-based system to automate this process**. It will take organisation applications as input, help identify organisations that are suitable for funding, and provide relevant recommendations to other organisations on how they can improve.
