# Plan of Action: Week 6 to Week 8

**Cohort:** 4 students, 2 from 3rd semester (Allen House), 2 from 5th semester (Maharana Pratap)

**Module mapping:** Week 6 = Guardrails and Observability (last content module), Week 7 = Capstone Week 1, Week 8 = Capstone Week 2. Actual capstone building happens in Weeks 7 and 8. Week 6 is used to finish the last topic and prepare students to start building right away after.

## Friday, 18 Sep 2026

Send a WhatsApp message to the group asking students to prepare their synopsis as per the earlier mail, and to share their GitHub repo link in the group as well.

## Saturday, 19 Sep 2026 (Week 6)

**Morning:** Theory and lab session on Guardrails and Observability. Finish the lab the same day. Where possible, connect the examples to each student's own project, for instance asking them what could go wrong in their own agent and how a guardrail would stop it.

**Afternoon:** Each student presents their synopsis. Give feedback right after each presentation. Let students fix their synopsis the same day based on that feedback, so nothing carries over unfinished.

## Sunday, 20 Sep 2026 (Week 6)

Each student gives a final presentation of their revised synopsis. Confirm that each synopsis is complete and ready to build from, that their GitHub repo is created, and that their tools or API keys are ready, so they can begin coding right away in the build week.

Also inform the group of the final date for the capstone PPT presentation, which is Saturday, 26 Sep 2026.

## Monday 21 Sep to Friday 25 Sep 2026 (Build Week)

Students build their capstone project on their own, based on the finalized synopsis.

Send one WhatsApp check in message midweek, for example on Wednesday 23 Sep, asking each student to share their progress and GitHub repo link. This is a light check in, not a class, and helps you catch anyone who is stuck before Saturday.

## Saturday, 26 Sep 2026 (Week 7)

Each student presents their capstone project through a PPT. Give feedback on what is working and what still needs fixing. Right after the feedback, help each student fix and improve their project the same day.

## Sunday, 27 Sep 2026 (Week 7)

Each student gives their final PPT presentation. Record this session, since this is the version you may use later for portfolio and course promotion.

After the presentations, help each student with deployment of their project, so it runs properly outside of just their own laptop.

## Monday 28 Sep to Friday 2 Oct 2026 (Between Week 7 and Week 8)

Students complete any remaining deployment work and final fixes.

Send a WhatsApp check in message on Tuesday and Thursday asking each student to share their progress and any blockers.

## Saturday, 3 Oct 2026 (Week 8)

Students complete deployment of their project pipeline. Try to get each student to finish their demo session and complete the recording the same day. If someone has not managed to deploy, note clearly what is left for them to finish on their own.

## Sunday, 4 Oct 2026 (Week 8)

This day is kept for any changes needed after Saturday's demo and recording. If a student's demo needs fixing or the recording needs to be redone, complete that on this day. There are two outcomes overall. Either the student has completed their project, or they have not. If someone has not finished, note clearly what is missing so they know what to complete on their own after the program ends.

## Logistics Needed

Only two things are needed for this plan, a projector for the PPT presentations and a tripod for recording. No other equipment is required.

## Recording Plan

On 27 Sep (final PPT presentation) and 3 Oct (deployment), take normal photos and record video while each student presents or demos their work. Keep it simple, just capture it as it happens.

Share the photos and recordings on the same day with the students and the team, rather than holding onto them for later.

Send one message in the WhatsApp group before 27 Sep letting students know that photos and video will be taken during the presentation and deployment sessions for internal review and course promotion. This covers consent so you do not need to ask again later.
